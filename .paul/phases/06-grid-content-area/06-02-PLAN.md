<objective>
## Goal
Add a **masonry/waterfall** layout and a **view-mode switch** (Grid ↔ Masonry) to the content
area. The toolbar gets a segmented view-mode control; the grid renders either the existing
uniform `VirtuosoGrid` (Grid mode) or a virtualized `VirtuosoMasonry` that lays images out at
their natural aspect ratio (Masonry mode). The thumbnail-size slider and sort from 06-01 drive
both modes.

## Purpose
Masonry is the centerpiece of Phase 6 — Eagle's signature browse experience. This plan delivers
it while keeping 60fps at scale via virtualization (decision: use `@virtuoso.dev/masonry`, the
Virtuoso-family masonry component). It extends — does not replace — 06-01's `useGridView` +
`ContentToolbar`, filling the view-mode slot the toolbar reserved.

## Output
- New dependency `@virtuoso.dev/masonry` (virtualized masonry).
- `useGridView` extended with a persisted `viewMode: 'grid' | 'masonry'`.
- `ContentToolbar` gains a Grid/Masonry segmented control.
- `useElementWidth` hook (ResizeObserver) → drives masonry `columnCount` from container width + thumbSize.
- `Grid` renders `VirtuosoGrid` or `VirtuosoMasonry` by mode; masonry cells use real aspect ratios.
</objective>
